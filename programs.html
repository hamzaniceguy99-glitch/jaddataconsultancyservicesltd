Program 02 · Built around a decision

Reporting and dashboards

A dashboard with forty tiles gets ignored within a month. We start from the decisions somebody actually makes weekly, agree a written definition for every measure, and build the smallest thing that answers them — because disagreement about what a number means is what kills reporting, not the chart type.

  • Organisations
  • Fixed per phase

Program 03 · Same answer every time

Pipelines and warehousing

Manual extracts produce a different total depending on who ran them and when. Automating that removes the variation, and the work is mostly in the error handling — what happens when a source is late, a field changes type, or a load half-completes. Silent failures are worse than loud ones.

  • 4 to 10 weeks
  • Organisations
  • Fixed per phase
  • System access
